# Connex Lab v0.1.0

First playable Android prototype of the Connex construction simulator.

### Included
- classic rod family from green through gray with measured physical lengths;
- seven classic planar connector shapes with 45° socket topology;
- end/socket snap building;
- 90° connector-to-rod cross snap;
- through-hub axle placement with free axial slide and rotation;
- 45° twist control for building out of plane;
- geometry auto-closing to compatible existing connectors;
- mobile orbit + pinch zoom controls;
- Undo and build-pose reset;
- Build/Simulate rigid-body physics toggle with an anchored root connector;
- procedural rod and connector visuals; no copied official meshes/assets.

### Fidelity scope
v0.1 models the core connection kinematics and rigid-body physics. Rod flex, snap pull-out force, detailed contact friction/tolerance, special blue/purple 3D interlocks, gears, motors, chain, wheels and specialty parts are future work.

### APK signing
The attached APK is debug-signed for direct sideload/testing. It is not a Play Store production-signed package.
