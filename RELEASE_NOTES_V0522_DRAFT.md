# Connex Lab v0.5.22

This release focuses on Android camera controls and editor UI density.

- Joystick movement and UP/DOWN elevation now work simultaneously with independent touch IDs.
- UP/DOWN controls are much larger; the joystick is slightly larger and shifted away from the editor sidebar.
- Touch viewport navigation is now one-finger orbit only: pinch zoom and two-finger pan are disabled.
- TRANSFORM keeps rotation rings and significantly longer translation arrows together, while the right helper card is smaller and non-collapsible.
- The top bar is now an icon toolbar with Select, Center, Undo/Redo, Simulate/Restore, confirmed Restart/Delete, and Options.
- Options promotes Physics, Save & Load, and Help; Structure Rigidity moves into Physics and is included in Reset Physics.
- Persistent menus gain top-right close buttons.
- The bottom parts bar uses compact navigation icons and live procedural previews for the selected rod and connector.
- Existing SOCKET, AXLE, CROSS, O-Ring, save/load and simulation behavior is retained.
